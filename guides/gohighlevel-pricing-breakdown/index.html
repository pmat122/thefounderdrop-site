The Hidden Costs: What GHL Charges Beyond the Subscription

This is the section most GoHighLevel reviews skip, and it is the section that matters most for accurate budgeting. GHL's base plans are flat-rate, but several critical features carry usage-based costs.

SMS and Phone Usage

GHL uses Twilio (or LC Phone, their own telephony layer) under the hood for SMS and phone calls. You pay per message and per minute.

  • SMS (US): Approximately $0.0079 per segment sent, $0.0079 per segment received. A standard 160-character text is one segment. Longer messages split into multiple segments.
  • MMS: Approximately $0.0200 per message (messages with images or media).
  • Phone calls: Approximately $0.0140 per minute for outbound calls in the US.
  • Phone number: $1.15 per month for a local number, $2.00 per month for a toll-free number.

What this costs in practice: A solo founder sending 500 SMS per month and making 100 minutes of calls spends roughly $5 to $8 per month on telephony. An active agency running SMS campaigns for clients might spend $30 to $100 per month. These are not hidden in the sense of being secret, but they are easy to overlook when you see the $97 headline price.

Email Sending

GHL gives you two options for email delivery:

  • LC Email (GHL's built-in provider): Approximately $0.675 per 1,000 emails. Simple to set up. Good deliverability out of the box.
  • Mailgun (third-party integration): Approximately $0.80 to $1.00 per 1,000 emails depending on your Mailgun plan. Better control over sending domains and IP reputation for high-volume senders.

What this costs in practice: A solo founder sending 5,000 emails per month pays $3 to $5. A business running larger campaigns at 20,000 to 50,000 emails per month pays $14 to $50. Compare this to ActiveCampaign or Mailchimp where you are paying $49 to $149 per month for similar volume — the usage-based model is dramatically cheaper for most founders.

Custom Domain

You need a custom domain to host your funnels, landing pages, and booking links on your own URL instead of a generic GHL subdomain. This is not a GHL charge — it is a standard domain registration fee of $10 to $15 per year through any registrar. If you already own a domain, you just point a subdomain (like go.yourdomain.com) to GHL.

A2P 10DLC Registration (for US SMS)

If you are sending business SMS in the United States, you need to register your brand and campaign through the A2P 10DLC system. This is a one-time registration fee of approximately $4 for brand registration plus a recurring $1.50 per month per campaign. This is not a GHL markup — it is an industry-wide requirement imposed by carriers to reduce spam. GHL walks you through the registration process inside the platform.

Why $97 Is the Sweet Spot for Solo Founders

The Starter plan is not a stripped-down teaser designed to push you toward the $297 tier. It is a genuinely complete platform. Here is why it works for solo founders specifically.

Unlimited contacts means your cost does not scale with your list. Mailchimp charges $20 per month for 500 contacts and $100 per month for 5,000. ActiveCampaign charges $49 per month for 1,000 contacts and $149 for 5,000. With GHL, your 50th contact and your 5,000th contact cost the same: zero incremental dollars. For a solo founder building a list over time, this eliminates the anxiety of watching your email bill climb as your audience grows.

Everything is connected without integration tax. The funnel captures the lead. The CRM stores them. The automation nurtures them. The calendar books them. The pipeline tracks them. The review system follows up after delivery. In a separate-tool stack, every one of those handoffs requires Zapier, a webhook, or a native integration — each one a potential break point. In GHL, they are one system. For a solo founder without a tech team, this is not a convenience. It is a survival requirement.

SMS is included, not bolted on. For service businesses — coaches, consultants, contractors, agencies — text messaging is the highest-converting follow-up channel. SMS has 98% open rates compared to 20% for email. Most CRMs and email platforms do not include SMS at all. With GHL, you text a lead from the same inbox where you email them, and it all attaches to their contact record. No separate Twilio account. No custom code.

You can build funnels without ClickFunnels. The $97 GHL plan includes a funnel builder that handles opt-in pages, sales pages, booking funnels, and order forms. It is not as polished as ClickFunnels. When to Upgrade: The Decision Framework

Stay on Starter until one of these becomes true. Then upgrade to exactly the plan that matches your new reality.

Upgrade to Unlimited ($297/mo) When:

  • You start managing client accounts. The moment you onboard your first client and need a separate CRM, pipeline, and automation environment for their business, you need sub-accounts. That means Unlimited.
  • You are running multiple businesses or brands. If you own two companies with different audiences, different funnels, and different pipelines, separate sub-accounts keep the data clean.
  • You need API access. If you are building custom integrations, syncing with proprietary tools, or programmatically managing contacts and workflows, the API is only available on Unlimited and above.
  • Your team exceeds three people. Starter caps at three users. When your fourth team member needs access, upgrade.

Upgrade to SaaS Pro ($497/mo) When:

  • You want to sell GHL as your own software. SaaS Pro lets you white-label the entire platform and sell subscriptions to your clients under your brand. You set the pricing. They never see GoHighLevel's name.
  • You want to rebill usage costs with a markup. SaaS Pro automatically charges clients for their SMS, phone, and email usage at rates you define. This turns your $497 subscription into a revenue-generating SaaS product.
  • You are building a productized service business. Instead of selling done-for-you marketing, you sell access to a pre-configured marketing platform. Higher margins, less labor, recurring revenue.

If none of those scenarios describe you right now, stay on Starter. The $97 plan is not a stepping stone. It is the foundation. Most solo founders will run their business on Starter for years without hitting a ceiling.

What GHL Does Not Replace

GoHighLevel is comprehensive, but it is not everything. Being honest about what you still need alongside GHL prevents buyer's remorse and helps you budget accurately.

Lead sourcing and prospecting. GHL manages leads once they are in your system. It does not find them for you. For building targeted prospect lists — filtering by industry, company size, funding stage, and job title — a prospecting tool like Apollo fills the gap. Apollo lets you build lists of decision-makers matching your ideal customer profile and export them directly into your CRM for outreach. GHL nurtures and converts the leads. Apollo finds them. They complement each other naturally — Apollo at $49 to $99 per month for the leads, GHL at $97 per month for the system that converts them.

Accounting and invoicing. GHL processes payments via Stripe for products sold through funnels and order forms, but it is not accounting software. You still need QuickBooks, Xero, or FreshBooks for invoicing, bookkeeping, and tax reporting.

Project management. GHL manages client relationships. It does not manage project delivery. You still need Asana, Notion, ClickUp, or a similar tool for task and project tracking.

Advanced analytics. GHL's reporting covers pipeline metrics, campaign performance, and call tracking. For deeper business intelligence, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or a dedicated BI tool fills the gap.

The point is not that GHL replaces everything. It is that it replaces the five to eight marketing, sales, and communication tools that typically account for $300 to $600 of a solo founder's monthly software spend. The tools it does not replace — accounting, project management, analytics — are tools you would need regardless of which CRM you choose.
